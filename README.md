# Privacy Policy for Funny Reader

**Last Updated**: November 22, 2024

## Overview

Funny Reader ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the Extension handles data.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The Extension:
- Does NOT track your browsing history
- Does NOT collect personal information
- Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
- Does NOT transmit any data to external servers
- Does NOT share any information with third parties

## Local Storage

The Extension uses Chrome's local storage API to store:

1. **Toggle State**: Whether the extension is enabled or disabled
2. **Donation Banner Timing**: Timestamp of when the donation banner was last shown (to ensure it only appears once per 24 hours)

This data:
- Is stored ONLY on your device
- Is NOT transmitted anywhere
- Is NOT accessible to us or anyone else
- Can be cleared by removing the extension

## Permissions

The Extension requests the following permission:

- **Storage**: Used to remember your enable/disable preference and donation banner display timing

The Extension does NOT request permissions for:
- Browsing history
- Personal data
- Network access for data transmission
- Cross-site tracking

## Third-Party Services

The Extension includes an optional donation banner with a link to Buy Me a Coffee (buymeacoffee.com). This link:
- Only opens when you click it
- Is clearly labeled and dismissible
- Does NOT automatically share any data
- Only appears once per 24 hours on non-whitelisted sites

When you click the donation link and visit Buy Me a Coffee, you are subject to their privacy policy, not ours.

## Content Modification

The Extension modifies text content on web pages you visit by replacing certain words with humorous alternatives. This modification:
- Happens entirely in your browser
- Does NOT send page content anywhere
- Does NOT store what you're reading
- Is completely local to your device

## Whitelisted Domains

The Extension automatically disables itself on certain domains (search engines, documentation sites, educational resources) to avoid interfering with your work. The list of whitelisted domains:
- Is stored in the extension code
- Does NOT report which sites you visit
- Simply prevents the extension from running on those sites

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there are no special considerations for children's data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by the Extension:

1. Right-click the extension icon
2. Select "Remove from Chrome"
3. Confirm removal

All locally stored preferences will be deleted immediately.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please:
- Open an issue on our repository
- Contact us via the Chrome Web Store listing

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data:
- There is no personal data to request
- There is no personal data to delete (beyond removing the extension)
- There is no personal data to correct
- There is no personal data to export

The Extension is designed with privacy-first principles: **we can't misuse data we never collect**.

## Compliance

This Extension complies with:
- Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Other applicable privacy laws

Our compliance is straightforward: we simply don't collect any data.

---

**Summary**: Funny Reader collects zero personal data. Everything happens locally in your browser. We believe in your privacy.
